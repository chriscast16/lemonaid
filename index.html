About Lemonaid

Lemonaid helps used-car and first-time buyers check a vehicle before they buy it. We combine live public data from the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) with practical inspection guidance.

  • Decode vehicle specifications straight from the VIN via NHTSA vPIC.
  • Check live safety recalls and understand risks and remedies in plain English.
  • Bring a 10-step checklist and essential seller questions to your test drive.
ℹ
Data source: NHTSA public datasets (vPIC, Recalls, and Complaints). No personal information, passwords, or accounts are used. Always arrange an independent mechanic inspection before buying.
